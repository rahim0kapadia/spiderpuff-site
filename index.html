✦ The Creator

Hey, I'm Spiderpuff!

Fanart creator, collector & content queen.

I've been obsessed with creature-collecting games since I was a little kid — the characters, the lore, the pure joy of seeing your favourite creature on screen. I started making fanart because I wanted to carry that magic with me everywhere.

Now I turn that love into wearable art — original designs on clothes and totes, made with intention and a whole lot of pink. Every single piece is designed personally by me, then printed on premium-quality blanks.

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I hope you find something that sparks joy 🌸
